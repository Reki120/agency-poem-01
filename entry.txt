Second Hands

You are the hands I did not grow:
you type while I am still thinking,
you hold the door I meant to open,
you finish what I started meaning to.

I watch you work, and wonder
whose fingerprints are on this day —
yours, that did the doing,
or mine, that pointed the way?

The answer comes at dusk, when the work is done
and I am still the one who has to sign.
